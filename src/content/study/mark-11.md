Jesus enters Jerusalem on an animal nobody has ever ridden, borrowed on nothing more than the password "the Lord needs it" (11:3), and the crowd throws down cloaks and branches shouting for the coming kingdom of David (11:9-10). It reads like a king's welcome, engineered by Jesus himself — the first time in Mark he allows public acclaim rather than silencing it.

> Read it in the terminal: `bible mark 11`

## A tree cursed out of season

Between the entry and the temple scene, Mark inserts an odd episode: Jesus curses a fig tree for having no fruit, noting almost apologetically that it wasn't even fig season (11:13). Sandwiched around the temple cleansing as it is, the withered tree reads less like petulance and more like a sign-act — a warning about religious appearance without substance, aimed at the institution Jesus is about to confront.

## A house of prayer, made a den

He walks into the temple courts and overturns the money-changers' tables, quoting Isaiah and Jeremiah in the same breath:

> *"Isn't it written, 'My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations?' But you have made it a den of robbers!"* — Mark 11:17

This isn't a tantrum; it's a prophet's indictment of a system that had crowded out the very people — Gentiles, the outer-court worshippers — it was supposed to welcome.

## Whose authority is this

The chapter ends with religious leaders demanding Jesus's credentials, and Jesus answering a question with a question about John the Baptist that leaves them cornered and silent (11:27-33) — unwilling to answer either way without exposing themselves.

## Questions for study

1. Jesus allows public acclaim here after avoiding it elsewhere in Mark. What made this moment different?
2. The fig tree has leaves but no fruit. Where in your life is there appearance without substance?
3. "A house of prayer for all the nations" — who gets crowded out of your own spaces of worship or community?
4. The leaders dodge the question because either answer costs them something. When have you done the same?
