Luke pairs two scenes on purpose. Jesus leaves the Jordan "full of the Holy Spirit" and is "led by the Spirit in the wilderness" to be tested for forty days. Then he returns to Galilee "in the power of the Spirit" and walks into his hometown synagogue. Fullness of the Spirit doesn't exempt him from hunger, from temptation, or from a hostile crowd — it walks him straight into both.

> Read it in the terminal: `bible luke 4`

## Three offers, one answer

The devil's three tests all aim at the same shortcut: skip the suffering, take the power now. Turn stone to bread. Take the kingdoms in exchange for worship. Force God's hand from the temple roof. Each time Jesus answers from Deuteronomy — not with a clever counter-argument but with a settled loyalty that doesn't need to be argued into existence.

## Today, in your hearing

In Nazareth, Jesus reads Isaiah's promise of good news to the poor and freedom for captives, then sits down and says, "Today, this Scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing." The crowd is amazed — briefly. Once he presses the point that God's mercy has always reached past Israel to outsiders, the same room that admired him tries to throw him off a cliff.

## Casting out and settling in

The rest of the chapter moves fast: a demon shouting Jesus's identity in a synagogue, Simon's mother-in-law healed of a fever and immediately serving, crowds pressing in at sundown for healing. When they try to keep him, Jesus explains why he can't stay — he was sent for this, and it isn't finished in one town.

> *"It is written, 'Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.'"* — Luke 4:4

> *"The Spirit of the Lord is on me, because he has anointed me to preach good news to the poor."* — Luke 4:18

## Questions for study

1. Why does Luke emphasize that the Spirit leads Jesus into testing rather than only into ministry?
2. What is the common thread in the devil's three offers?
3. Why does Jesus's sermon in Nazareth provoke murder rather than repentance?
4. Where do you see the pattern of "welcomed, then rejected once the cost is clear" playing out today?
